# G2-C policy resolver

Issue #16 adds deterministic preflight for native, remix, and bridged turn proposals. The implementation starts from main at e89bf17fc8c65e3e0e07733e369bc8abe629f5c7. The world-policy, turn-resolution, and state/VM contracts remain authoritative.

`resolvePolicy(snapshot, proposal)` validates two versioned inputs and returns an effect plan or a rejection with an evidence trace. `PolicyResolverPort` is the richer handoff for #18. The #14 `PolicyPort` and #14/#15 wire schemas are preserved. Snapshot and proposal enter through separate channels. The coordinator supplies trusted snapshot evidence and verifies authorization, identity, freshness, and actual effects again at commit. Schema validation cannot authenticate snapshot data.

- The snapshot supplies committed rules and retired lineage, trusted mode and trigger, a region catalog, field ownership and dependencies, evidence states, and optional exact native capability evidence. Missing, unmapped and contradictory evidence remain distinct from confirmed absence. Time windows are inclusive at the start and exclusive at the end, using the supplied overlay turn.
- Registered operations derive effects before comparing them to the exact declared set. Inventory transfers check holder and both containers. Connections touch both endpoints and follow their transitive dependencies. Surface coverage, ownership, and risk come from the snapshot. Rejection returns no partial plan. Operations that write another operation's inputs require sequential resolution on fresh state.
- Play synthesis needs an allowance for each affected write. A matching deny wins, and unknown region membership cannot establish an allowance or dismiss a possible deny or invariant. Authored native input is resolved as a single Play subturn. Editor writes require exact proposal authority and still observe applicable denies, invariants, and capabilities.
- Native bindings are keyed by canonical operation digest and refer to a manifest entry with verified story, runtime, adapter, checkpoint format, pending-input type, effect coverage, and recovery. Named manifest preconditions need matching structured predicates and current evidence. The resolver-specific manifest extends the operation vocabulary for synthetic inventory, connection, and dialogue capabilities. Cross-owner operations need a registered bridge covering both footprints. A claimed takeover requires verified suppression or interception of every declared conflicting native surface.
- Structural guards support the registered possession and unchanged-field checks. Adapter and semantic judgments come from trusted evidence tied to the same request and exact operation. A consequential uncertain judgment blocks. A low-impact, reversible, unprotected semantic judgment may proceed as best effort, with its uncertainty recorded in the trace.
- Editor amendments are recompiled from trusted compiler context and the submitted interpretation and selection. The exact #15 wrapper, including source records, clause dispositions, and lineage, is preserved in the plan. A full proposal with unresolved clauses requires supported-subset selection. Validated replacements keep retained pieces and inherit rule time windows. Current generation restrictions remain in force for the candidate's one-time patches. The coordinator owns applying the candidate and persisting lineage.
